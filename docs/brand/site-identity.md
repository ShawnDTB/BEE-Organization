# Works by BEE identity — September 2026

The latest owner direction is Brian's welding-arc reference supplied by Shawn: blue-white light, electric blue, violet and occasional yellow-orange. It supersedes the earlier copper/bone palette for the current site. The public domain is worksbybee.com. Public presentation: Works by BEE; legal entity: BEE Organization LLC.

The core narrative is **Build. Empower. Equip.** Build an identity, empower a community, equip your people. Apparel and embroidery must be obvious to a nontechnical buyer. Primary audiences are groups/businesses, creators/communities and individual custom projects.

Dark neutrals carry the interface. Blue-white highlights and blue actions establish the identity. Violet is supporting light. Amber is optional and restrained. No constant flashing, excessive glow, mascot/honey imagery or dominant honeycomb. Product photography and accurate work samples should ultimately carry the visual proof.

Space Grotesk headings and Inter body/UI remain in use. Use readable labels and controls, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and a native mobile dialog. Desktop navigation begins above 1180px; the menu closes at the same boundary.

Shawn authorized an original identity after launch because Brian has no current logo. The active wordmark is public/brand/bee-wordmark.svg: bold chamfered BEE letterforms with substantial electric-blue center strokes in the E letters. It is an original vector direction, not a reconstruction of a previously approved asset. Light and dark monochrome variants and bee-symbol.svg accompany it. The earlier modular B remains archived for historical references, not the header/favicon.

Use the color wordmark on carbon/graphite, the dark monochrome on light material, and the light monochrome on dark material. Maintain at least one stem-width of clear space. Suggested starting sizes for review: 90px wordmark on screen, 24px symbol. Do not stretch, add glow, bevel, outline, or place on a busy photograph. Tiny print and embroidery need a physical test and appropriate digitization; the SVG is not a stitch file. Trademark/name clearance and Brian's final approval remain separate business tasks.

The shared source is src/styles/site.css. It consolidates the previous 11 global stylesheets and removes obsolete component selectors. Existing active Studio/service rules remain. Future work should use shared tokens and components rather than append a new broad override sheet.

Do not invent customer work, partnerships, sales, turnaround, prices, testimonials or production readiness. Quotes, original request snapshots, approved proof revisions and paid receipts must remain distinct records.
