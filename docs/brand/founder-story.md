# Founder Story Foundation

BEE Organization LLC takes its initials from founder **Brian Eugene Everson**.

Brian is a hands-on builder with interests spanning custom apparel production, embroidery, graphic garments, gaming, computers, and hardware. The brand should reflect that practical maker mindset without forcing every customer interaction into a technology theme.

The customer-facing story should focus on what that background means for the buyer:

- Careful setup
- Organized production
- Respect for materials and equipment
- A willingness to solve unusual requests
- A system that can be improved over time

This story should be expanded only with details Brian personally approves.
