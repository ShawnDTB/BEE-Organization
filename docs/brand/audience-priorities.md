# Audience Priorities

## Primary: bulk organizations

Schools, teams, clubs, businesses, churches, nonprofits, events, and community groups.

They need:

- Clear quantity pricing
- Dependable deadlines
- Size and garment organization
- Proof approval
- Consistent quality
- Simple reordering

## Secondary: creators and personal brands

Streamers, video creators, musicians, gaming communities, and emerging brands.

They need:

- Strong design presentation
- Small test runs
- Consistent brand files
- Product photography
- Featured launches
- Reorder and fulfillment support

## Supporting: individual custom work

Gifts, memorial apparel, family events, birthdays, and one-off designs.

This work remains available but should not obscure the bulk and creator positioning that can create repeatable revenue.
