# Launch Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Confirm public name and naming clearance
- Confirm products, equipment, production limits, garment vendors, and decoration methods
- Finalize DTB and BEE responsibilities and commercial agreement
- Approve identity direction
- Photograph existing work and equipment

## Phase 2 — Identity and prototype

- Refine the Engineered Monogram
- Produce embroidery and transfer tests
- Finalize typography and color tokens
- Design homepage and quote-flow prototype
- Draft policies and sales materials

## Phase 3 — Platform build

- Build marketing pages
- Build guided quote intake
- Add secure artwork uploads
- Add email notifications and structured order records
- Add portfolio and creator features

## Phase 4 — Commerce and operations

- Add proof approval
- Add deposits and approved-order payments
- Add order status and reorder workflow
- Add internal production views and analytics

## Phase 5 — Launch and case study

- Accessibility, performance, mobile, SEO, and security review
- Content and production-photography population
- Domain deployment
- Launch reporting
- DTB case study documenting brand, platform, workflow, and measurable outcomes
