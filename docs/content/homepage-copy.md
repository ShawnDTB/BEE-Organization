## Process

1. Tell us what you are making.
2. Select garments, quantities, colors, and placement.
3. Upload artwork or request design assistance.
4. Review pricing and approve the proof.
5. Production begins after the required payment.
6. Receive the order and reorder without starting over.

## Trust statement

Every custom order should have a documented garment choice, decoration method, approved proof,
quantity, size breakdown, production target, and pickup or shipping plan before production begins.
