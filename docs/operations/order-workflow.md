# Quote-to-Order Workflow

## Customer intake fields

- Customer type: group, creator, business, school, event, or individual
- Contact name, organization, email, and phone
- Garment category and preferred brand/style
- Quantity range and size breakdown
- Garment color
- Decoration method: embroidery, graphic transfer, or guidance needed
- Decoration locations
- Artwork upload and artwork-readiness status
- Requested completion date
- Pickup or shipping preference
- Notes, names, numbers, or personalization requirements

## Workflow stages

1. New inquiry
2. Information review
3. Garment and production recommendation
4. Quote sent
5. Quote accepted
6. Artwork prepared
7. Proof sent
8. Proof approved
9. Deposit or payment received
10. Materials ordered
11. Production queued
12. Production in progress
13. Quality control
14. Ready for pickup or shipment
15. Complete
16. Follow-up and review request
17. Reorder eligible

## Reorder requirements

Returning customers should provide the prior order number and only identify changes to quantity,
sizes, garment color, personalization, deadline, or delivery. The approved production artwork
should remain stored under a stable customer and project record.

## Controls

- No production without written proof approval.
- Deadline commitments must account for garment availability and production capacity.
- Customer-supplied artwork should preserve the original file and production-ready derivative.
- Any garment substitution requires customer approval.
- Refunds, cancellations, misprints, sizing, and customer-supplied garments require written policies.
